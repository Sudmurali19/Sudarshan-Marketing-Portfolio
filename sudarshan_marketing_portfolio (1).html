Case Study 01
Content Workflow Governance Across Product, Marketing & Compliance
+
Problem Statement

Digital campaign content had to pass through multiple teams — product, marketing, and compliance — before it could be published. Without a structured workflow, content was getting delayed, reviewed inconsistently, and sometimes published without full sign-off, creating real regulatory risk in a SEBI-regulated environment.

Client Brief

Axis Securities needed a reliable system for content to move across teams without losing brand alignment, missing compliance checks, or slowing down campaign timelines. The goal was speed and safety, not a trade-off between them.

My Contribution
  • Coordinated content flow across product, marketing, and compliance stakeholders
  • Structured and maintained the approval process for multiple simultaneous campaigns
  • Implemented checklists to reduce publishing errors and enforce review discipline
  • Ensured all content stayed aligned with brand guidelines and campaign objectives
Impact
5+ Simultaneous Campaigns Governed
3 Teams Aligned
~40% Reduction in Review Cycles
0 Compliance Escalations
What this demonstrates: I understand content operations, quality control, and multi-stakeholder management. I don't just write content — I understand what it takes to get content through a complex organisation safely and on time.

Case Study 06
Content QA Checklist for Campaign Publishing Risk Reduction

In a regulated BFSI environment, publishing content without a complete review is not just an editorial risk — it's a compliance risk. Multiple campaigns were running simultaneously, and the review system lacked the structure to catch errors consistently before they went live.

Client Brief

Build a review system that catches errors at every stage of the content lifecycle — from draft to compliance review to final publish — without slowing down the team's execution pace.

My Contribution
  • Designed and implemented a content QA checklist for campaign execution
  • Mapped review stages across editorial, brand, compliance, and publish
  • Reduced publishing errors across multiple campaign releases
  • Made the review process repeatable and less reliant on memory
Impact
10-point QA Checklist Designed
5+ Campaign Releases Covered
~30% Reduction in Publishing Errors
Reusable Process Across Campaigns
What this demonstrates: I bring process thinking to content work. Most content people focus on the output; I focus on the system. A good checklist isn't bureaucracy — it's how you protect the quality of your content at scale.
